# Business and qualified legal review checklist

**Unpublished review packet. No item is approved merely because this file exists.** Owner assignments below are proposed responsibilities, not named appointments. Requested company facts remain unanswered as of this audit.

| Decision / evidence needed | Responsible decision-maker | Release evidence required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Responsible registered entity, incorporation/registration particulars, country and postal address; verify control/delivery of support, sales, privacy and grievance channels | Founder + counsel | Approved identity/contact text and test delivery; decide public business address rather than inventing one. |
| Initial countries, merchant type, shopper audience and whether children are allowed; geographies excluded and how exclusion works | Founder + counsel | Jurisdiction/role analysis and operationally enforceable launch scope; no default assumption of adults-only or worldwide availability. |
| Account controller vs merchant processor role for each purpose; metadata analytics and fraud roles | Privacy owner + counsel | Processing record, Article 28/other applicable DPA, merchant notice/consent responsibilities and assistance procedure. |
| Legal basis / purpose per data category, upload notice and right to withdraw; separate optional analytics from service processing and contract acceptance | Counsel + privacy/engineering | Approved notices and versioning; do not equate agreeing to a privacy policy with consent to all uses. |
| Retention for accounts/OTP/email/feedback, consent/security/financial/quota/event records, catalog images and backups; legal holds and statutory duties | Counsel + finance + security | Signed category schedule, automation and rehearsal. Preserve immutable chain and accounting integrity while minimizing personal fields. |
| Hard retention promises vs age thresholds/scheduler downtime; no-training statement and absence of image export across all processors | Engineering + counsel | Idle/crash/restart/live deletion, log/storage/backup checks and vendor restrictions; approve exact wording only after proof. |
| Shopify uninstall/redaction: retained revoked key identifiers, accounting and unsettled quota, removed personal/store data | Counsel + engineering | Updated truthful public text and mandatory webhook test; do not promise full financial-record deletion. |
| Production vendors, regions, support/subcontractor access, transfers, guarantees and subcontractor change notice | Founder + counsel + operations | Signed/accepted terms and DPAs, transfer mechanism assessment and populated subprocessor register. |
| Access/export/correction/erasure/grievance intake, identity checks, owner, legally applicable deadlines, refusal/hold reasoning and appeal path | Counsel + privacy owner | End-to-end synthetic request drill, case register and mailbox delivery. Avoid asking for a photo or identity document by default. |
| Prices, currency, tax/GST, included usage, quotas, failed/unknown-job treatment, recurring renewal, cancellation, refund/duplicate-charge/outage handling and trial conversion | Founder + finance + counsel | Approved commercial decision record and checkout/portal/invoice walkthrough. **No changes authorized by this audit.** |
| Customer assent and policy version records, including OAuth, liability, remedies, eligibility, governing law and forum | Counsel + founder | Approved terms and durable versioned acceptance design respecting mandatory rights. |
| CatVTON, base model/VAE and all supporting models/code/data; contractor ownership, source licences/NOTICE, logo and demo person/garment rights | Founder + qualified IP counsel | Sufficient signed permissions and exact-artifact register before commercial use. A free business pilot is not automatically noncommercial. |
| Marketing reference outputs, 0.95 metric meaning, latency targets, hardware wording and any future conversion/return claims | Product + founder + counsel | Rights-cleared claims register with benchmark IDs/conditions. Missing evidence is not a finding of fabrication. |
| India IT Act/SPDI transition, DPDP commencement/corrigenda, CERT-In reporting/logs and grievance requirements; consumer/e-commerce applicability and 2027 amendments | Indian counsel + security | Dated interpretation and tested incident/log procedure; do not use future DPDP commencement to ignore current obligations. |
| UK DUAA/PECR exceptions/commencement, EEA GDPR/ePrivacy/DPA/transfers, California adjusted thresholds and other targeted states | Relevant counsel | Market-specific approval, not a blanket worldwide-compliance statement. |
| India SGI/intermediary role and EU AI Act Article 50 provider/deployer transparency, marking and exceptions | Relevant counsel + model/product owners | Current operative text and reviewed output-disclosure/provenance design; validate Standard realism before changing output. |
| Applicable accessibility duties, EU EAA scope/exemptions and actual accessibility statement | Counsel + accessibility reviewer | Manual assistive-tech and authenticated/SDK evidence; no conformance declaration from automated scan alone. |
| Independent penetration test scope/authorization, remediation and retest; pilot participants/rights/outcomes | Founder + security + independent tester | Exact environment/commit reports, no open high/critical findings, controlled pilot exit and founder approval. |
