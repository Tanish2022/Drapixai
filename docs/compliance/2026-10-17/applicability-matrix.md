# Jurisdiction-specific applicability matrix

Reviewed 17 October 2026. **Conditional engineering assessment for counsel, not a legal opinion.** Actual entity, establishment, launch countries, customers, visitor volumes and vendor locations are not yet confirmed. B2B subscriptions do not remove duties concerning identifiable merchant staff or shoppers. Public availability alone does not settle territorial targeting. Recheck authoritative consolidated texts, amendments and commencement at the actual launch date.

| Jurisdiction / requirement | Legal basis and applicability test | Current implementation / gap | Action |

| India: digital personal data | [DPDP Act commencement, G.S.R. 843(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf). Scope covers qualifying digital processing in India and specified overseas offering. Substantive provisions are phased, not all already operative on this review date. | Photos, account data and identifiable metadata fall within the review; entity and processing roles unresolved. | Prepare notice, purpose, rights, children, deletion, safeguards and processor controls; counsel map each provision to the launch date. |
| India: DPDP Rules timing | [Rules 2025, G.S.R. 846(E), rule 1](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf): rules 1, 2, 17–21 on publication; rule 4 after one year; rules 3, 5–16, 22–23 after eighteen months. | October 2026 is before the one-year and eighteen-month milestones under the reviewed notification. | Do not claim all DPDP duties are presently in force or use the transition as a privacy waiver. Verify publication-date computation, corrigenda and later amendments with counsel (milestones November 2026 / May 2027). |
| India: existing IT/SPDI protection | [IT reasonable security and SPDI Rules 2011](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/494931), including privacy, consent, security, grievance and transfers where the body-corporate/SPDI conditions hold. Passwords are relevant; a photo is not automatically identification biometric data merely because a person appears in it. | Bcrypt passwords, photos, access controls and draft privacy notice exist; no verified responsible officer or transfer contracts. | Counsel confirm current IT Act section 43A/Rules transition and data classifications. Identify owner, notice, access/correction/withdrawal process and documented safeguards. |
| India: incidents and logs | [CERT-In directions and FAQ](https://www.cert-in.org.in/Directions70B.jsp), section 70B directions: assess service-provider/body-corporate scope and listed incidents; incident reporting, point of contact, time synchronization and specified logs. | Runbooks and immutable audit controls exist; operational delivery, incident clock and qualifying log retention/India location unverified. | Confirm applicable six-hour reporting and 180-day ICT-log requirements against the official directions. Distinguish minimized security logs from shopper images; do not retain photos to satisfy a log rule. |
| India: consumers / e-commerce | [Consumer Protection Act/rules index](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/consumer-protection-acts). Determine whether DrapixAI sells to statutory consumers and acts as an e-commerce entity; merchant software vendor is not automatically the merchant's marketplace. | USD price pages, hosted billing and current refund text exist. Commercial purpose and business model classification need counsel. | Approve identity, grievance route, cancellation/refund/tax disclosures and trial/renewal journey. No commercial text changed. |
| India: forthcoming e-commerce amendment | [Government announcement, 10 September 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=294532) reports amendments commencing 1 January 2027. | Future launch timing may cross commencement. Scope and operative Gazette require counsel review. | Calendar a launch-date recheck; assess grievance, dark-pattern, discount and other provisions only where relevant. Do not invent a compliance certificate. |
| India: synthetic media / intermediary role | [MeitY 2026 SGI FAQ](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/10/065b6deb585441b5ccdf8be42502a49c.pdf) describes amendments commencing 20 February 2026. Coverage depends on intermediary role and legal SGI definition/exclusions. | Generated-preview terms exist; persistent label/provenance and unlawful-content controls not fully assessed. | Confirm operative consolidated rules and any later changes. Counsel decide labels/provenance and safeguards; review image-quality impact before output changes. Consultation drafts are not final law. |
| EU/EEA: GDPR | [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng), especially Arts 3, 5–6, 12–22, 25, 28, 32–35, 44 onward. Trigger may arise through establishment, targeted offering or monitoring; processor contract obligations can also flow from EU merchants. | Merchant/shopper roles, purposes/bases, DPA, DPIA screening, representatives, vendors and transfers unresolved. | Confirm intended markets and roles by processing activity. Document notices, appropriate bases, rights, processors, incident workflow and transfer safeguards where applicable. No claim of GDPR compliance. |
| EU/EEA: terminal storage | [ePrivacy Directive, as amended, Art 5(3)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:02002L0058-20091219) and applicable national implementation. Nonessential identifiers and embeds require purpose/consent assessment; not limited to cookies. | Analytics opt-in/withdrawal now implemented; necessary sessions remain operational; video is user-loaded. | Validate deployed vendor inventory and local rules. Consent is not a substitute for an analytics retention schedule. |
| UK: UK GDPR and PECR | [ICO territorial scope](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/personal-information-what-is-it/who-does-the-uk-gdpr-apply-to/), [current storage guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-the-pecr-rules/), [DUAA changes](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/data-use-and-access-act-2025-factsheets/data-use-and-access-act-factsheet-pec-regulations). Scope/commencement and exceptions require current review. | Same role/notice/transfer gaps; conservative opt-in is used rather than assuming analytics qualifies for an exception. | Determine UK targeting, current exceptions, UK transfer mechanism and any representative duty. No blanket assertion that every analytics technology always needs consent under current UK law. |
| California: CCPA/CPRA | [California AG applicability guidance](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa). For-profit California business plus applicable revenue, buying/selling/sharing or revenue-source thresholds; also evaluate service-provider/contractor obligations. | California activity, adjusted revenue threshold, qualifying counts, affiliated control and contract status unknown. | Obtain threshold/role facts; evaluate notices, rights, sale/sharing/GPC and contracts if triggered. Do not declare inapplicable because the company is new or B2B. |
| Other US states / children | Target-state laws, deceptive-practices, breach, biometric and child-specific rules depend on facts. No conclusion of universal US coverage or exemption. | Markets and minimum age undecided. No verified parental consent/age-assurance workflow. Ordinary photo processing must not be mislabelled anonymous or uniquely identifying biometric processing without evidence. | Counsel scope state thresholds, COPPA/teen and biometric duties before serving relevant audiences or adding identity recognition. Avoid collecting birth dates or identity documents without a justified design. |
| Accessibility | [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) AA is the requested technical target; legal duties depend on market, service and exemptions. | Keyboard/support/skip improvements made; whole-site/SDK conformance not established. | Automated plus manual keyboard, screen reader, 200–400% zoom, 320px reflow, contrast and mobile testing. Counsel separately assess India, EU EAA, UK equality and US accessibility duties. |
| EU: accessibility scope | [EAA, Directive 2019/882](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/882/oj) covers specified products/services from 28 June 2025, with scope, exemptions and national implementation conditions. | B2B software and shopper-facing merchant e-commerce require separate role/scope assessment; microenterprise status not confirmed. | Counsel assess actual service and exemption facts; WCAG AA work alone is not a legal EAA declaration. |
| EU: AI transparency | [Commission Article 50 guidance](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/guidelines-ai-transparency-obligations) addresses duties applying from 2 August 2026. Scope depends on provider/deployer role, generated content, exceptions and any applicable transition. | Service generates realistic preview images; durable marking/provenance and applicable disclosure duties not established. | Counsel map exact obligations and operative dates to actual EU offering; review compliant marking without degrading Standard realism. |
| Intellectual property / AI representations | Exact code/model/content licences, trademarks and image/personality rights; [CatVTON upstream](https://github.com/Zheng-Chong/CatVTON) identifies a noncommercial licence. | Commercial permission and complete asset chain of title absent from evidence. | Obtain rights and counsel approval for exact releases and demo media. Preserve Standard quality while resolving rights. |

The matrix distinguishes a technical target from a legal obligation and current obligations from future commencement. It must be signed off against actual company facts and deployed processing before release.
