## India DPDP and conditional jurisdiction review

Rechecked official [Act text](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf),
[commencement notification G.S.R. 843(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf)
and [Rules G.S.R. 846(E), rule 1](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf).
Commencement is phased; 18 October 2026 precedes the one-year and eighteen-month
milestones in those notifications. Do not state that all substantive DPDP duties
are already operative, or that the transition waives existing Indian duties.
Counsel must check operative amendments/corrigenda and launch-date requirements.

Engineering preparation addresses clear purposes/notices, separate choices,
minimization and withdrawal. It does not settle lawful grounds, controller/
processor roles, child/guardian handling, grievance ownership, rights deadlines,
incident reporting, vendors/transfers or retention schedules. Those decisions
need qualified review of the actual business and deployment. See the existing
[jurisdiction matrix](2026-10-17/applicability-matrix.md) for conditional Indian,
EEA/UK, US, consumer and AI/transparency issues; no worldwide compliance claim.

An opt-in analytics control is appropriate for this site's optional identifier
and unknown target markets; it is not a claim that India requires a generic
cookie banner on every site. [ICO storage guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/what-are-storage-and-access-technologies/)
distinguishes permitted exceptions from consent requirements. Any jurisdictional
exception needs its own scope/conditions review; this change does not rely on one.
Accessibility checks target the [WCAG reference](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/)
and [modal keyboard pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/).
